## Definitions and limitations

- AOV and NAOV/nAOV are different measures. Preserve the company's reported definition.
- Contribution margin and Adjusted EBITDA margin are different metrics.
- GOV and NOV are different denominators. Do not compare margin percentages without accounting for the denominator.
- These public figures will inform assumptions for a separate synthetic dark-store model. They are not measurements of that synthetic store.
- Orders per store per day for Blinkit is a derived estimate, not a directly reported company metric.
